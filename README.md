# Retail Store Expansion Pro-Forma | Dollar General

**Author:** Anagha Athreyas
**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Dollar General Annual Report FY2024

---

## Objective

To evaluate whether opening a new Dollar General store meets the company's capital investment criteria — replicating the type of analysis a corporate Financial Planning and Analysis team performs before approving real estate capital expenditure.

---

## Why Dollar General

Dollar General opens more new stores than any other US retailer — 730 new stores in FY2024 alone. Every single opening requires a capital investment decision backed by exactly this type of pro-forma analysis. Their Annual Report provides transparent store-level data that makes the model fully anchored in real, auditable numbers.

---

## Model Structure

| Sheet | Contents |
|---|---|
| Assumptions | All inputs in one place — change any cell to update the full model |
| 5-Year Pro-Forma Profit and Loss | Revenue, gross profit, operating expenses, EBITDA, and free cash flow by year |
| Net Present Value, Internal Rate of Return, Payback | Capital investment decision metrics against a 10% hurdle rate |
| Scenario Analysis | Bear, base, and bull cases across revenue and margin assumptions |
| README | Project brief, data sources, and key findings |

---

## Key Assumptions (Base Case)

- Store size: 7,400 square feet
- Revenue per square foot: $259 (Dollar General Annual Report FY2024)
- Gross margin: 30.2%
- Annual lease cost: $120,000
- Total initial investment: $455,000
- Discount rate: 10%

---

## Key Findings (Base Case)

- Year 1 Net Revenue: ~$1.42M
- Year 1 EBITDA: ~$145K
- 5-Year Net Present Value: positive at 10% discount rate
- Internal Rate of Return: ~18%
- Payback period: ~3.5 years
- Base case clears the hurdle rate. Bear case does not — location quality is the swing factor.
